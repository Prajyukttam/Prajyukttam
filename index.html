"Welcome to the annual technical fest!"

Prajyukttam, Mercemoc, and Arthavardhana form an extraordinary convergence of technology, business strategy, and economic innovation—bringing together tech enthusiasts, aspiring managers, and creative entrepreneurs on a single dynamic platform.

This multi-faceted celebration is designed to challenge the mind, foster collaboration, and showcase talent across three distinct yet interconnected domains:

About The Fest

Prajyukttam, Mercemoc, and Arthavardhana form an extraordinary convergence of technology, commerce, and management, empowering the next generation of innovators, strategists, and leaders.

  • Prajyukttam (Technology Festival): Celebrates innovation, engineering, and coding through robotics, hackathons, and startup showcases to inspire tomorrow’s technocrats.
  • Mercemoc (Commerce Festival): Fosters financial literacy, entrepreneurship, and market acumen with real-world business simulations and marketing challenges.
  • Arthavardhana (Management Festival): Focuses on leadership, decision-making, and strategic vision through case studies and business plan competitions to cultivate future industry leaders.

Together, this multi-disciplinary fest blends technical brilliance, financial expertise, and managerial foresight into a single platform for excellence and impact.
